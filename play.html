How to Play

Goal: Your creatures eat pixels from the environment. Reconstruct the motif image by digesting at the right time. Reach the SSIM score target (e.g. 0.7) to complete the level.

Controls:

  • Q Split — Trigger reproduction for all \"pregnant\" cells (energy above threshold).
  • W Burst — Short speed and attraction boost.
  • E Evolve — Let creatures adapt their colors toward the environment.
  • D Digest — Use eaten pixels to form one reconstruction. Each digest locks those pixels (they can’t be reused).
  • R Respawn — New random spawn and colors. Does not restore digests or eaten pixels.
  • Space Pause / resume

UI: Top bar shows level name, cells, time, and the eaten pixels circle with its counter. Reconstructions you keep appear as small cards under the circle.

Win by getting one digest with SSIM ≥ target. If you use all digests without reaching it, the motif fails and you can load the next one.
